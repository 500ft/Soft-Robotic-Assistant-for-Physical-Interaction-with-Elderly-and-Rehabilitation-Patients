## Limits and retained work

Source annotations and calculations are completed documentation, not independent experiments. The selected task, force envelope, moment arms, support reactions and release behavior remain unmeasured for this proposed device. Existing safety, clinical, standards and patent statements require qualified review before they guide a new device programme. This candidate makes no certification, clinical benefit or freedom-to-operate finding.

The current NEXT_STEPS file is retained history/planning and is not executed or reauthorized by this review. No purchase, build, human contact or new experiment is authorized.

## Three continuation directions

1. Adopt a specific low-force task and instrumented bench concept, with a grounded load path and defined release behavior.
2. Measure the assumed moment arms and characterize force, displacement and fault response before freezing device requirements.
3. Obtain the relevant institutional and specialist review before any human interaction or clinical interpretation.

These are possible separate continuation directions, not an adopted development schedule.
